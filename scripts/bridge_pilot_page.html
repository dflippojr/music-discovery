How this works
  • Each pair has one song you know and two tracks from the Free Music Archive, A and B. Pick the one that sounds closer to the song.
  • Give each track about a minute. That is about 3 minutes a pair, or 1.5 hours in all. Stop whenever you like: every pick saves as you make it.
  • Judge by ear. Ignore the genre, tags, artist and play counts on the archive's page, because they could give away how a track was chosen.
  • If a page is gone or a track won't play, choose "Can't compare". Don't search for the track elsewhere.
  • You can change a pick until you finish. There is no right answer to chase, and a close call still gets a pick.
